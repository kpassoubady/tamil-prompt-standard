# Book Cover Image Prompts — v1.0 Showcase Edition

**Book:** The Art of Commanding AI in Tamil — Showcase Edition
**Author:** Kangeyan Passoubady (Kangs) | Kavin School
**Size:** 6 × 9 inches (trade paperback) — use portrait aspect ratio 2:3
**Tool:** Google Imagen (Gemini website)

---

## Front Cover Prompt

```
A premium trade paperback book cover design, portrait orientation 2:3 aspect ratio.

Central visual: The Tamil letter "அ" (the first letter of the Tamil alphabet, symbolising the beginning of knowledge) rendered as a luminous, three-dimensional glyph floating in deep space. The letter glows with soft electric-blue and gold light, with fine neural-network circuit lines radiating outward from it like synapses, connecting to small floating dots of light.

Surrounding the central glyph: seven or eight fragments of Tamil script text gently orbiting — they appear as glowing code, semi-transparent, like data flowing through an AI system. The script should be legible but soft-edged, as if projected in holographic light.

Colour palette: Deep midnight navy (#0A0E2A) background, electric teal (#00C8C8) circuit lines, warm gold (#D4AF37) Tamil letterforms, soft white light halos. Rich, jewel-toned and modern.

Lighting: Dramatic single-source light from behind the central "அ", creating a subtle lens flare. The overall feel is cosmic and expansive.

Foreground text layout space: Leave the lower quarter of the image slightly darker and clear for title typography overlay. Leave the top fifth clear for a subtitle band.

Style: Cinematic sci-fi meets classical Tamil manuscript aesthetics. Ultra-detailed, 8K, professional book cover illustration, digital art, no humans, no faces.

Mood: Awe-inspiring, intellectual, culturally rooted yet futuristic.
```

## Back Cover Prompt

```
A premium trade paperback back cover design, portrait orientation 2:3 aspect ratio, designed to complement a deep midnight navy front cover.

Background: Deep midnight navy (#0A0E2A) gradient fading slightly lighter towards the centre, matching the front cover palette. A very subtle, low-opacity texture of fine Tamil script characters fills the background like watermark wallpaper — barely visible, giving depth without distraction.

Left side visual accent: A slim vertical column of soft teal-and-gold circuit lines runs down the left edge (about 10% width), mirroring the neural-network motif from the front cover.

Centre content zone: A large, clear rectangular area (approximately 80% of the cover width, 65% of the height) with a very slightly lighter navy background — this is where the book blurb and author bio text will be placed. Keep this area completely clean with no decorative elements inside.

Bottom section:
- A thin gold horizontal rule divides the blurb area from the bottom strip.
- The bottom strip (bottom 15% of cover) has a slightly darker background for the barcode, publisher logo (Kavin School), and ISBN area — keep this completely clean and undecorated.

Top section:
- A thin teal horizontal rule at the top.
- Small space above the rule for the series name / category label.

Style: Minimal, elegant, professional. Matches the front cover's cinematic sci-fi meets Tamil manuscript aesthetic. Ultra-clean layout, 8K, professional book back cover, digital art.

Mood: Authoritative, trustworthy, modern Tamil tech publishing.
Do not print any other characters in english or the color
```

## Usage Notes

- Generate at **2400 × 3600 px minimum** (or highest available resolution) so it prints cleanly at 6 × 9 in at 400 DPI.
- For the front cover, after generating, overlay the title text in post (Canva / Figma):
  - **Title:** "The Art of Commanding AI in Tamil" — large, bold, gold or white
  - **Subtitle:** "Showcase Edition" — smaller, teal
  - **Author:** "Kangeyan Passoubady" — bottom, clean white, smaller
  - **Publisher:** "Kavin School" — very small, bottom
- For the back cover, the content zone is left clean for text overlay.
- Both images should be saved as **PNG** (not JPEG) to preserve sharp Tamil script edges.
