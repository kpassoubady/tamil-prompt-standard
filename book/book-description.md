# Amazon KDP — Book Description

**Book:** The Art of Commanding AI in Tamil
**Author:** Kangeyan Passoubady
**Target field:** Amazon product description (KDP → Bookshelf → Edit book details → Description)

---

## Language Decision

**Use English-primary with strategic Tamil phrases.** Reasons:
- Amazon's search and ranking algorithm is English-first — Tamil-only descriptions have poor discoverability even for Tamil readers
- The Tamil diaspora (US, UK, Canada, Singapore, Malaysia) searches in English
- A Tamil closing line and key Tamil terms signal authenticity without hurting reach
- Do NOT write a Tamil-primary description for KDP — it will not surface in search results

---

## About This Book (Showcase Edition)

This is the **showcase edition** — it contains curated sample prompts from all 10 domains (not the full 700+ collection). The complete open-source library of 700+ templates is freely available on GitHub. The description below is honest about this: it positions the book as a curated showcase that gives readers immediate hands-on value, while pointing to the full library.

## Plain Text Version

*(Copy-paste ready — no formatting)*

Are you speaking to AI in English — but living your life in Tamil?

This book changes that.

"The Art of Commanding AI in Tamil" is the first structured, community-built guide for getting precise, high-quality responses from AI systems — in Tamil. Whether you are a doctor explaining a diagnosis, a farmer asking about crop disease, a student preparing for board exams, or a poet looking for inspiration, this book gives you curated, ready-to-use Tamil prompts designed for your world.

Built on the Tamil Prompt Standard — an 8-layer framework developed from TNC21 / Tamil Research Chair on Artificial Intelligence research — every prompt is structured for clarity, cultural accuracy, and safety. All templates work with any modern AI: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Llama, and others.

This showcase edition covers all 10 domains:

- Healthcare (சுகாதாரம்): Patient explanations, clinical notes, AYUSH medicine, mental health support
- Education (கல்வி): Grade 1 through competitive exams, lesson planning, study strategies
- Agriculture (விவசாயம்): Crop management, government schemes, rural entrepreneurship
- Law & Governance (சட்டம்): Legal drafting, policy analysis, citizen rights
- Technology (தொழில்நுட்பம்): Code generation, data analysis, UI/UX design
- Business, Employment, Daily Life, and Literature

Each prompt follows the 8-layer taxonomy — Role, Domain, Skill Level, Intent, Tone, Format, Constraints, and Safety — so AI responses match your audience, register, and professional context. Pure Tamil (தனித்தமிழ்), Formal, Casual, and Tanglish tones are all covered.

Safety is built in. Healthcare, legal, and finance prompts carry mandatory Tamil-language disclaimers so you can use AI responsibly.

The full library of 700+ prompt templates is available free and open-source at github.com/kpassoubady/tamil-prompt-standard — this book is your curated starting point.

Authored by Kangeyan Passoubady (Kangs), Principal Automation Architect and founder of Kavin School LLC, with 28 years of software engineering experience — and a lifelong commitment to making AI accessible to Tamil speakers everywhere.

தமிழில் AI-யை கட்டளையிடுங்கள். உங்கள் மொழியில், உங்கள் துறையில், உங்கள் வாழ்க்கைக்கு.
